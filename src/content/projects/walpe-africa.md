### Brief

A website for the Women's Academy for Leadership and Political Excellence (WALPE) Africa, an organisation that trains, mentors, and protects women pursuing political leadership across the continent's five regions.

The site presents the organisation's mission, programmes, and leaders, and gives supporters a way to get involved.

The site is live at [walpeafrica.org](https://walpeafrica.org).
