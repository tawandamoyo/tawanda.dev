Artificial Intelligence (AI) is all the hype currently, and for good reason. It is powerful technology that's only getting better and poised to change the world in ways we can't even imagine. But how much of it is hype? What are the real-world applications of AI?

Here are some use cases that I have personally come across in the wild:

### Transcribing

- AI is very good at transcribing audio to text. This is very useful for anyone who needs to transcribe interviews, podcasts, or any other audio content. I've come across a few startups such as Recall.ai that leverage this technology to provide an API that interacts with meeting and collaboration services such as Zoom, Google Meet, and Slack. 

### Translation

- AI has also become very good at translation. Nowadays translation is one of the most used applications of AI. Google Translate is a good example of this. It is now trivial to translate text from one language to another, and even to translate the graphics on an image. 
- I recently was able to operate a device with a Japanese manual using AI

### Analysis

- Given some data AI can be used to analyze it and extract insights. At Policy Engine AI is used to analyze policy impacts.

### Summarization

- Another common use case of AI is summarization. AI can be used to summarize long articles, books, or any other text. This is useful for people who want to extract the salient parts of a text without reading the whole thing. 

### Search

- AI is also very good at searching through large amounts of data. It's also increasingly being used to personalize and contextualize search results, as well as to provide recommendations. Search engines such as Perplexity.ai are using AI to provide better search results, and the experience is much better than traditional search engines.
