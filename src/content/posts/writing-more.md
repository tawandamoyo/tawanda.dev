With AI now capable of writing entire essays in minutes, what value is there anymore in writing, especially the kind of writing such as this?

I have thought about this quite a bit lately, and my conclusion is that it's even more important to write now than it ever was. Why?

Well, for starters, writing helps in thinking and while this has always been important, I think it's even more important now that there are machines capable of writing for us. If we never have to write, to construct arguments, or lay down facts and ideas logically beyond the length of a tweet, what shall happen to our thinking?

Secondly I think increasingly the internet will be filled with AI generated content. Human created content will be a tiny drop in the ocean of AI stuff. Part of me wants to rebel against this, and for this reason I write. 

Writing is creation. Most people are consumers and not creators. I think there should be a balance between consuming and creating, preferebly with more creation. Given how abundant information has become, there are endless opportunities for consumption - from Tik Tok, to YouTube, to Twitter and Netflix. And yet the act of creation is more rewarding that consuming. Man, says Aristotle, is a maker.
