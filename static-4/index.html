For years I have made my living building software and explaining it — websites, tools, documentation. Lately most of my attention has settled on one question: as AI systems grow more powerful, who can see what they can do, what they run on, and who quietly loses the ability to say no? My answers take the form of essays and public, inspectable artifacts; the current one is the Africa Compute Tracker, and there will be others.

I am not in a hurry about this. I build things, make them public, and write down what I learn.
