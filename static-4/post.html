AI and the Web

2 April 2025 · web · ai

Artificial Intelligence (AI) will lead to dramatic shifts in how we use the internet. It will even change how the web itself is built, and how it works.

Here's what I think are some of the ways in which AI will change the web and how we browse it:

  • Most browsing will mostly be done by AI agents so browsers (and content) will have to be built or optimized for agents not humans. Companies like unternet are already thinking about how to construct an "AI native web."
  • AI might bypass the traditional web altogether. A paper suggests that in underdeveloped countries where data is expensive, it's cheaper to use AIs than the traditional web.1 As a result users in such countries effectively ignore/bypass the web altogether.
  • Most people will not search the web directly, instead relying on AI tools to do the searching (like what Perplexity is doing).
  • AI generated content will vastly outnumber human created content. As a result, even though there will be way more information, finding relevant information will become harder. It is possible that human generated content will become more valuable.
  1. "Cost, not capability, is the binding constraint" — the paper's finding, not mine; worth checking against local data prices before citing it as settled. ↑
